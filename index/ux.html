Jet's Pizza Ordering

USER: Miguel | STAKEHOLDER: Jet's Pizza

CASE: Miguel is a guest shopper in the Ypsilanti, MI area. He likes to order pizza on Friday evenings and tries the variety offered in Ypsilanti. As a guest shopper, he prefers to order online without registration and pick up his order to reduce costs. Price and reward clarity are important for Miguel, since he is a full-time student and works a part-time job. So, Miguel wants to know what he pays for and how much..
