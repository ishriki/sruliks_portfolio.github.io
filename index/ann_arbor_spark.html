Challenges & Solutions

1. Creative Tools & Budget

  • Challenge: Making high-quality visuals for an early-stage startup without a budget for expensive software subscriptions.
  • Solution: Researched affordable and free design tools to produce professional graphics and videos without adding software costs.

2. Setting Clear Goals

  • Challenge: Navigating fast-changing startup priorities and short deadlines.
  • Solution: Met directly with the founder to clarify my role, align on key priorities, and map out a simple plan to hit our target date.

3. Staying Ahead of Deadlines

  • Challenge: Managing a busy posting schedule across several social media platforms at once.
  • Solution: Created content in batches and used automated scheduling tools to keep posts going out on time.

4. Keeping Leadership Updated

  • Challenge: Sharing daily progress without clogging up calendars with extra meetings.
  • Solution: Sent concise daily email updates and quick Loom video walk-throughs so the founder could give fast feedback.

5. Getting the Word Out Fast

  • Challenge: Building initial brand awareness from scratch in a limited timeframe.
  • Solution: Focused on strong content output—designing 150+ custom graphics and videos and publishing over 120 posts.
